## Your desktop becomes a place.

Each workspace gets a home in the world: a wall in a sunlit court, a suspended screen in a hall, a console room in a frozen vault. Switch workspaces and you travel there. Your applications stay real applications: a terminal on the Sun Court wall takes your keystrokes, and a window you carry to another area stays live. The world supplies the setting, the shell supplies the controls, and Super+Tab hands you an ordinary cursor whenever you want one.

## Lumen Reach

The official release world is a working settlement on the terraces of a mountain watershed. It has four homes: **Sun Court** for arrival and field work, **Array Hall** for making and testing, **Rime Vault** for maintenance and archives, and **Signal Rise** for communications. Geometry and materials are original; the package is CC-BY-4.0.

## Early, and honest about it

Work on Hyprune started on 7 October 2026; it is an early preview. Core, shell, SDK, schemas and two worlds work together end to end in isolated nested sessions, and they have run in short supervised sessions on real hardware. Hardware-cursor sign-off on a real DRM session, an extension host and outside contributors are still ahead. The component repositories are not public yet; these docs, the RFCs and the schemas are.
