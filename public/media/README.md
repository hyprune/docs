# Media

`lumen-reach/` holds in-engine captures of **Lumen Reach**, Hyprune's official
release world, by Hyprune contributors, licensed **CC-BY-4.0** (see the world's
ATTRIBUTION.md in hyprune/worlds). Files are named `<view>-<world version>`.
`video/` holds short in-engine clips of the same world; each has a `.webp`
poster frame.

Stills were encoded to WebP at 800 and 1600 px wide (the hero also at full
capture width, WebP and AVIF) and clips to H.264 MP4 at 1280 px, ahead of time rather than at site
build time. Only Lumen Reach and the public Switchyard test world may appear
here.
