## Your desktop becomes a place.

A quiet room for deep work. An observatory for your projects. A studio with windows where you left them. Hyprune is an open foundation for desktop experiences that have space, presence, and a sense of place.

Your applications stay real applications. Your world supplies the setting. Your shell supplies the controls. You choose how to move between them—and can always return to the ordinary desktop.

<CardGrid stagger>
  <Card title="Worlds with purpose" icon="rocket">Author original environments in glTF, place surface anchors, and give everyday work a spatial home.</Card>
  <Card title="A desktop, still" icon="laptop">Bring live application windows into the world, with deliberate focus, familiar input, and a dependable exit.</Card>

## Early. Building in the open.

Hyprune is at its foundation stage. These RFCs describe the intended system; they are not a claim that the runtime or extension ecosystem is ready. We are starting with one original world, safe compositor integration, and a small, testable protocol.
